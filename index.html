Maxime Fournes

Co-founder of Pause IA · Speaker on AI risk

I spent over a decade working in artificial intelligence as a data scientist and machine learning engineer. Seeing how fast the field was moving, and how little was being done to manage the risks, I left industry to work full-time on AI safety advocacy.

I co-founded Pause IA, the French branch of PauseAI, a movement calling for an international pause on the development of the most powerful AI systems until we know how to make them safe.

I'm available for interviews, talks, panels and conferences, in French or English.
